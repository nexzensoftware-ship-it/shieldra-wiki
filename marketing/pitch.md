
**30-Second Opening**
Shieldra helps healthcare organizations and healthcare SaaS companies manage HIPAA and SOC 2 compliance without drowning in spreadsheets, scattered documents, and expensive consulting cycles. Most compliance tools were built for large enterprise security teams. Shieldra is built for teams that need practical, continuous compliance: upload documents, identify gaps, manage vendors, track risks, collect evidence, and generate audit-ready reports from one platform.

**The Problem**
Most companies are handling compliance in a broken way today:

- Policies live in Google Drive, evidence lives in Slack, risks live in spreadsheets, and vendor documents live in someone’s inbox.
- Traditional GRC tools are expensive, complex, and often designed around SOC 2 first, not healthcare workflows.
- Consultants can help, but they usually give a point-in-time report. The moment the business changes, the compliance picture becomes outdated.
- Teams do not know what is missing until an auditor, customer, or incident exposes the gap.
- Small and mid-sized healthcare teams are expected to meet the same regulatory expectations as larger organizations, but without the staff or budget.

**What Shieldra Solves**
Shieldra turns compliance from a once-a-year scramble into a continuous operating system.

Instead of asking, “Are we ready for an audit?” teams can see their readiness every day. Shieldra shows what controls are in place, what policies are missing, which vendors need attention, which risks are open, and what evidence is already collected.

**Major Features To Highlight**
- **HIPAA and SOC 2 framework support**: Manage both frameworks from one platform, with controls, risks, evidence, and reports mapped clearly.
- **AI document scanner**: Upload policies, BAAs, security documents, or evidence and Shieldra identifies gaps against compliance requirements.
- **Risk register and risk assessments**: Track high-level business risks and detailed assessment findings without confusing users.
- **Vendor and BAA management**: Keep vendor records, agreements, risk levels, expiration dates, and SOC 2 documents organized.
- **Controls library**: Manage control implementation, testing, framework coverage, and remediation status.
- **Audit-ready reports and auditor packs**: Generate useful reports that auditors and customers can actually review, instead of dumping raw JSON and CSV files.
- **Incident and breach workflows**: Track incidents, timelines, response steps, and documentation in one place.
- **Training and policy acknowledgments**: Show who completed required training and who acknowledged policies.
- **Continuous compliance dashboard**: Give leadership a clear view of readiness, open gaps, and what needs attention.

**Why Shieldra Is Different**
Shieldra is not just another checklist tool. It connects the work: documents, vendors, risks, controls, evidence, reports, and remediation.
