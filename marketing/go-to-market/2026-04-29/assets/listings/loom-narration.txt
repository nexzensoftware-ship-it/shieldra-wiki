Small healthcare teams still end up managing HIPAA readiness across spreadsheets, PDFs, inbox threads, and vendor paperwork. ShieldRA was built to make that work less chaotic and a lot more actionable. The easiest place to start is the free HIPAA risk assessment. In a couple of minutes, a practice can get a readiness score, spot likely gaps, and see where to focus first. From there, ShieldRA helps organize the messy middle of compliance work: policies, evidence, business associate tracking, and breach response follow-through. The goal is not to pretend compliance runs on autopilot. The goal is to give lean healthcare teams, dental groups, clinics, and rehab providers a practical system to see gaps clearly, stay organized, and know what to do next.
