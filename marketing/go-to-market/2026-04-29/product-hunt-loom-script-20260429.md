## Tight single-paragraph version

ShieldRA was built for small healthcare teams that are still managing HIPAA readiness across spreadsheets, PDFs, inbox threads, and vendor paperwork. The easiest place to start is the free HIPAA risk assessment, which gives practices a quick readiness score and highlights likely gaps. From there, ShieldRA helps organize the messy middle of compliance work: risk assessment, evidence, policies, business associate tracking, and breach-response follow-through. We’re not pretending compliance runs on autopilot. The goal is to give lean healthcare teams a practical system to see gaps clearly, stay organized, and know what to do next.
