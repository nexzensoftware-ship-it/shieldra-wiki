# ShieldRA startup listings + Product Hunt pack

**Date:** 2026-04-10
**Purpose:** ready-to-use copy and prioritization for Product Hunt plus other listing sites that help with backlinks, visibility, and credibility

## Straight call

Yes, list ShieldRA on Product Hunt.

But the real value is:
- credibility
- backlink + discoverability
- a clean public profile
- a reusable asset pack for other directories

It is **not** the primary acquisition engine. For ShieldRA, the buyer-fit channels are still direct outreach, partner pages, useful compliance content, and operator communities.

## Positioning call for listings

Use this story consistently:

**ShieldRA helps small healthcare practices get a clearer handle on HIPAA readiness.**

Focus on:
- HIPAA risk assessment
- readiness / gap visibility
- policy and evidence organization
- BAA tracking
- breach-response workflow support
- small healthcare practices, dental groups, rehab providers, clinics

Avoid on directory listings unless proven:
- `HIPAA compliant`
- `on autopilot`
- `10x faster`
- `24/7 expert support`
- certification claims like `SOC 2 Type II`, `HITRUST`, `GDPR compliant`
- hard savings or time claims

## Other listing sites worth doing

## Tier 1 — do now

### 1. Crunchbase
**Why:** strong credibility signal, company discovery, durable backlink, useful for investor / partner / prospect research trails.

**Use it for:**
- company overview
- category tagging
- founder/company profile completeness
- clean backlink + legitimacy

### 2. Wellfound
**Why:** strong startup profile surface and credibility page even before active hiring matters.

**Use it for:**
- company profile
- startup positioning
- founder presence
- additional backlink / discovery surface

### 3. Product Hunt
**Why:** best-known public launch surface. Good for awareness, social proof, and a durable product page.

**Use it for:**
- launch visibility
- reusable social asset
- one canonical product page outside the main site

### 4. BetaList
**Why:** still useful for early-stage startup discovery and permanent listing exposure.

**Use it for:**
- early-adopter visibility
- another durable launch-page backlink

### 5. Launching Next
**Why:** easy startup directory submission, permanent listing, decent support value for backlinks and discovery.

**Use it for:**
- incremental visibility
- another indexable product page

### 6. Startup Stash
**Why:** startup-tool directory with product submission path. Better for discoverability and backlinks than for direct buyer volume.

**Use it for:**
- category presence
- long-tail discovery
- additional backlink surface

### 7. Uneed
**Why:** smaller than Product Hunt but clearly positioned as a launchpad with homepage visibility and SEO value.

**Use it for:**
- additional launch-style exposure
- another clean listing page

## Tier 2 — do after real user proof exists

### G2
Wait until there are enough real users to support reviews. Good long-term SEO and buyer intent, bad place to show up half-empty.

### Capterra
Same logic as G2. Useful once there is review gravity and cleaner category positioning.

### GetApp
Useful later with reviews and stronger category clarity.

### TrustRadius
Better once there is real customer proof, references, and category fit.

---

## Tier 3 — low priority or easy to misuse

### Generic AI directories
Most AI directories will send the wrong audience. They can create backlinks, but usually weak buyer fit for ShieldRA.

### Spammy mass-submit directories
Do not spray ShieldRA across junk directories just to chase DR. That is how you get low-signal backlinks and zero actual buyer attention.
